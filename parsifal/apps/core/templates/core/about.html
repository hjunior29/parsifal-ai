What is Parsifal?

Parsifal (parsifal-ai) is an open, self-hosted web application designed to support researchers and AI coding agents conducting secondary studies in Software Engineering. Maintained by Helder Junior and based on the original Parsifal by Vitor Freitas, this fork keeps the structured Planning, Conducting, and Reporting workflow while adding modern methodological, collaborative, and automation capabilities.

Everything runs on a single SQLite database on your own machine or server — your protocols, imported BibTeX studies, full-text PDFs, extracted evidence, and manuscripts never leave your infrastructure.

Supported Methodologies

Secondary studies require rigor and repeatability across hundreds or thousands of candidate papers. Parsifal adapts its protocol, checklists, screening rounds, and data analysis to three established Software Engineering methodologies:

  • Systematic Literature Reviews (SLRs) — following Kitchenham & Charters (2007) [1]: objectives, PICOC, research questions, keywords and synonyms, search strings, inclusion and exclusion criteria, weighted quality assessment with cut-off scores, evidence-backed data extraction, and full reporting.
  • Systematic Mapping Studies (SMSs) — following Petersen, Vakkalanka & Kuzniarz (2015) [2]: classification facets with reusable templates, keywording, known-paper test sets, the Petersen process checklist, facet frequencies, and cross-tabulation matrices.
  • Rapid Reviews (RRs) — following Cartaxo, Pinto & Soares (2020) [3]: practical context, practitioner collaboration and methodological concessions checklists, multi-round screening (titles → titles & abstracts → full text), and threats to validity drafted directly from concessions.

What this Parsifal adds

AI-Assisted Screening Exclude, include, or screen studies with AI (Jev) per screening round with per-criterion yes/no prompts, customizable probability thresholds, AI relevance scores, and resumable run logs.
Multi-Reviewer Agreement Independent per-reviewer decisions, Cohen’s kappa for any pair of reviewers, side-by-side disagreement inspection, and consolidation rules (unanimous, majority, any include).
PRISMA 2020 & Analytics Automatic PRISMA 2020 flow diagram [4] (including rapid-review round splits), selection funnel, publications per year and source, quality scores, and extraction/mapping charts.
Evidence & Traceability Every extracted value stores its supporting passage from the study, and a complete History log records every change (including protocol amendments) across Web, API, CLI, and MCP.
Reporting & Manuscripts Structured threats to validity with mitigations, versioned manuscripts with an in-page PDF viewer, full-text PDF storage (local or S3), and exports to DOCX, XLSX, CSV, and JSON.
API, CLI & MCP Server Every review operation is available via a JSON REST API (/api/v1/), the parsifal CLI, and the parsifal-mcp server for Claude Code, Codex, and other AI agents.
Self-Hosting & Backups Lightweight SQLite deployment with Docker Compose or Python, one-archive instance and per-review backups (database + PDFs), institutional library access (Via CAPES), and native light & dark themes.
