About the known studies

A test set is a list of studies you know the search should find. Petersen et al. (2015, §5.1.2) found it the most common way to evaluate a search: an expert in the area, for example, gives some ten papers that should be found; without an expert, the web pages of key researchers of the field may list them.

Each known study is looked for among the imported studies, by its DOI or else by its title, again after every import. The share found is the search's recall; a known study not found is worth a look at the search string and the sources.

It is optional: a review without known studies is complete without them.

K. Petersen, S. Vakkalanka and L. Kuzniarz, "Guidelines for conducting systematic mapping studies in software engineering: An update", Information and Software Technology, 64, 2015.
