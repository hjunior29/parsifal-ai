About the rapid review checklist

A rapid review cuts cost and time on purpose, but it is not an ad-hoc review: every concession made to be rapid is documented in its protocol, with why it was made. "Transparency is the golden standard."

The concessions are a menu, not a package: one rapid review keeps several sources and skips the quality appraisal, another uses a single source and appraises the quality rigorously. Tick what this review does, and justify it.

A concession brings a threat to validity. Draft the threats to validity adds one to Reporting for each concession ticked, with its justification as the mitigation; it only adds, and skips a threat the review has already.

B. Cartaxo, G. Pinto and S. Soares, "Rapid Reviews in Software Engineering", arXiv:2003.10006, 2020, §2.1, §4.1–4.3. B. Cartaxo, G. Pinto and S. Soares, "The Role of Rapid Reviews in Supporting Decision-Making in Software Engineering Practice", EASE 2018.
